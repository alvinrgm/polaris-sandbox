How it works

One loop, every week.

Each part feeds the next, so your plan keeps up with your life.

  1. Build your plan.

    Tell Polaris your goal and your week. Your training plan sets your food targets.

  2. Log your food.

    A photo or a sentence. Your targets move with what you did today.

  3. Train and get stronger.

    A demo for every lift, and a suggestion for what to lift next.

  4. Track your progress.

    Charts with your bests for every lift, and your week at a glance.

  5. Get ahead of small aches.

    Tap where it hurts, and rehab moves slot into your workouts so a sore spot doesn't stop your training.

My right forearm aches when I curl. What should I change this week?

A dull ache in the forearm muscle (brachioradialis) is common when getting back into lifting, especially with heavy gripping, pull-ups, or curls. Since you've lifted 3 times in the last month, it's likely muscle or tendon overload adapting to the work.

Questions

Before you start.

Is Polaris for someone like me?

Probably. Polaris is built for people just starting out, people who mainly want to track food, people working around an ache or injury, and serious lifters. It works for every gender, every body and every kind of training, and pregnancy and new moms have a place here too. See who it's for.

Does Polaris replace my PT or doctor?

No. Polaris works alongside your PT or doctor and keeps your rehab going every day between visits.

Where do your coach's suggestions come from?

Your coach uses your logged sets, meals, weigh-ins and tapped aches, plus your goal. It also follows set rules, like adding reps before weight, never going below a safe calorie floor, and moving through rehab in phases.

Can I bring my workout history?

Yes. Bring your history from another workout app's export file (CSV). It works today.
