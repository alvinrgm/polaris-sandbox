Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 November 2026 · Version 10. Polaris is made by RISE LLC, 13157 Mindanao Way, PMB 127, Marina Del Rey, CA 90292 · support@polarisbody.com.

This policy explains what Polaris collects, how it's used and shared, and your choices. Health data has its own page too: our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy.

What we collect

  • Account: your name, email and profile photo from Google sign-in. If you use Sign in with Apple, we get a sign-in ID from Apple and the name and email you choose to share; if you pick Hide My Email, we only see Apple's relay address.
  • Profile: sex, birth year, height, weight, goals, equipment and training days.
  • Health and body data: weigh-ins, waist and body fat %; aches and pain reports (where, how bad, and your answers to questions); rehab check-ins; pregnancy, due date, birth date and breastfeeding status; and any notes you type.
  • Training: workouts, sets, reps, weights, activities and plans, including training history you import from another app (for example a Hevy or Strong export file you choose).
  • Food: meals, amounts, calories and nutrients, and meal photos you take for an estimate.
  • Coach chats: what you ask the Coach and what it answers, and short notes the Coach keeps between chats about what you told it (for example a goal or an injury).
  • Notifications: what we've told you about in the app (the bell: for example friend requests, comments, kudos and celebrations, crew news and weekly results), and, only if you allow push notifications, your browser's push address and keys on the web app or your iPhone's push token in the iPhone app, so our server can send you reminders and updates about your training, your friends and your account. We keep which notifications we sent, by which channel (in the app, push or email), and whether you opened them, to avoid sending too many. Your browser's push service (for example Apple, Google or Mozilla) or Apple Push Notification service delivers each push and sees only the message. Some reminders, such as the monthly recap, are scheduled on your phone itself. Turn each kind on or off in Settings, or in your phone's notification settings; when you turn push off, we delete the push address or token.
  • Apple Health (iPhone app, only if you turn it on): the workouts (type, time, duration, distance and active energy) and body weight you allow Polaris to read, and the lifting sessions you finish, which Polaris saves to Apple Health. See Apple Health below.
  • Purchases: if you buy Polaris Pro or anything else from us, which plan or product you bought, the price, when it started and renews, whether it's a trial or used an offer or promo code, refunds, cancellations and billing problems, and your country, currency and, where tax law needs it, postal code. In the iPhone app, Apple handles the payment. If we sell on our website, a payment processor (for example Stripe) handles it and may give us your card's brand, last four digits and expiry date. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Friends, crews and the Feed: your display name, @handle and profile photo; friends and friend requests; posts, captions, comments, kudos and celebrations; your weekly results, rankings and badges (for example being #1 among your friends or in a crew); crews you start or join (name, goal, lift challenge and members) and nudges; your notifications and notification settings; reports you make or that are made about you, blocks, and any moderation action we take; and the share links you create.
  • Feedback: notes you send with Send feedback.
  • Usage: when you sign up, which days you open the app, which link brought you, the app version, feature-use events (for example "finished a workout"), session replays in the web app (see Mixpanel below), and a record of each AI request (which feature, which model and its cost) to run the free plan's limits and keep the Coach available.
  • Technical: error reports (browser or iPhone model, iOS version, app version and the screen that failed) and standard web server logs, which include IP addresses.
  • Website advertising and analytics: on our marketing website (for example polarisbody.com and polarisbody.com/food, not the app's health screens), unless you opt out, tags from Meta (Meta Pixel and Conversions API), Google (Google tag, Google Ads and Google Analytics) and TikTok (TikTok Pixel and Events API) can record the pages you visit, the ad or link that brought you, your browser and device, cookie and advertising identifiers, your IP address, and whether you joined the waitlist or signed up. These events never include your email or any health data.

We don't collect precise location or your contacts. The iPhone app doesn't use Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) and doesn't track you across other companies' apps or websites.

How we use it

  • To run Polaris for you: build your plan and food targets, show your progress, run the Coach and Physio, and let friends see what you choose to share.
  • To run your subscription: unlock Pro, run free trials, restore a purchase on a new phone, and keep the records the law requires for tax and accounting.
  • To keep it safe and working: apply safety limits to targets, read error reports, fix bugs and prevent abuse.
  • To improve the Coach: when the Coach's own checks catch a mistake, we save that chat with your name removed and no account ID for up to 30 days, to turn it into a test. Once a week we also review recent Coach chats to find mistakes and missing features. For that review, names, emails and phone numbers are removed and people are labeled P1, P2 and so on. The review stays private to the Polaris team.
  • To understand usage in aggregate: feature-use counts without health details, and session replays to see where the app is hard to use. You can turn this off.
  • Advertising: unless you opt out on our website, to measure our ads, build audiences of people like our users, and show you Polaris ads on other sites and apps. We reserve the right to show ads inside Polaris in the future. Ads in the app would never use your health data unless you opt in separately, and never use data from Apple Health at all.
  • To contact you: notifications in the app, by push and by email about your training, your friends and your account (for example a friend request, a comment, kudos or a celebration, a weekly win, a crew nudge or a reminder), according to your settings; service emails, including a few getting-started emails in your first weeks, receipts and billing notices, security notices and notice of changes to our terms; and, if you join the waitlist or opt in, product news and offers. Turn each kind off in Settings, or tap unsubscribe in any email. Some messages about your account, security, purchases or legal changes can't be turned off while you have an account.
  • To keep the community safe: review reports, hide or remove content, and limit or close accounts that break our Terms. We act on reports within 24 hours.

Sale and sharing for advertising, and your privacy choices

For visitors in the United States, advertising cookies are on by default on our marketing website, and you can turn them off at any time (see below). For visitors in Europe (EU/EEA, UK and Switzerland, based on the time zone your browser reports) and on our recovery page (polarisbody.com/recovery), advertising cookies stay off until you choose Accept. Sharing your website activity (pages visited, the ad that brought you, and whether you joined the waitlist or signed up) with Meta, Google and TikTok may count as a "sale" or "sharing" for cross-context behavioral (targeted) advertising under California law and similar laws in other states. In the last 12 months, the categories involved are identifiers (cookie, advertising and device IDs, IP address) and internet activity on our website.

You can opt out at any time: tap Your privacy choices (at the bottom of every page) and choose Disable ad cookies (or Reject, where you are asked first). We remember your choice on that browser, and the ad tags then stop loading. We treat a Global Privacy Control signal from your browser as an opt-out, and the ad tags then never load. Opting out doesn't change anything else about Polaris.

Health data is different. We never send the health data you keep in the app (workouts, food, body measurements, aches, rehab, pregnancy details or Coach chats) to advertising partners, and we never sell it, unless you give separate, explicit opt-in consent. Any sale of consumer health data would also need your signed authorization, as the law requires. Data from Apple Health is never sent to advertising partners or sold, even with consent. See our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy.

Who we share it with

We share data only with service providers that process it for us, and only what each one needs:

  • Supabase (database, sign-in and server functions): everything you store, to host the app.
  • Our AI provider: Google (Gemini API). When you use the Coach, Physio, a food estimate or your monthly recap note, or type an exercise name Polaris doesn't know yet, our server sends Google the parts of your log that the request needs (for example your profile, recent workouts, food, body measurements, aches or rehab, and pregnancy details if you've entered them, including workouts and weight that came from Apple Health), your message, any meal photo, and the exercise name to sort. Google uses it only to write the reply, estimate, plan, recap note or exercise match and send it back. Under our paid API terms, Google doesn't use it to train its models. We may switch to Anthropic (Claude API) on the same terms; we'll update this page when we do. Meal photos aren't stored by us. The app asks before anything goes to Google, naming what's shared, and you can turn AI on or off any time in Settings → Privacy → AI coach. Logging, your plan and your progress all work with it off.
  • Sentry: error reports and the feedback notes you send, to fix bugs and read your feedback.
  • Mixpanel: feature-use events with a random ID, never your name, email or health details, to chart how the app is used. In the web app (not the iPhone app, and not for people in Europe), also session replays: a recording of how you move through the app (which screens you open, where you tap and scroll), with the same random ID, and a note when a tap gets no response or is repeated quickly, which records only where on the page it was, never what the button says. Before anything leaves your device, every word and number on screen and everything you type is blanked out, pictures are removed, and the Physio body map and the pregnancy questions are left out entirely. Mixpanel keeps replays for 30 days. Turn both off in Settings → Data and privacy → Share usage analytics.
  • Loops: your email address, list membership, and which steps you've reached in the app (signed up, finished setup, logged something, first and second workout, or not logged in a while) and the first goal you picked (training, food, or all of it), to send you getting-started emails; and, for notification emails, the kind of update, the display name of the friend it's about (for example "Jo sent you a friend request") or that you were #1 that week, and a link back to the app. Never your workouts, weights, food, body measurements, aches, chats or the text of comments.
  • Push services: Apple Push Notification service (iPhone app) and your browser's push service (web app), which deliver push notifications and see only the message.
  • Payment processors: if we sell on our website, a payment processor (for example Stripe) handles your payment details and receives your name, email, billing country and postal code, and what you bought, under its own privacy policy. It never receives your health data.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, if you use it; the App Store, which handles Pro payments, trials, refunds and cancellations under Apple's Privacy Policy; and Apple Health, which stays on your iPhone (see below).
  • RevenueCat: manages Pro subscriptions in the iPhone app. It receives a random app user ID (not your name or email), your Apple purchase receipts and transactions, your app version, device type and country, and tells our server whether Pro is active. It never receives your health data. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.
  • Google: sign-in, and the YouTube player for exercise demos. Polaris uses YouTube API Services; see the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Advertising and analytics partners (website only, unless you opt out, or only after you accept where we ask first): Meta Platforms, Google and TikTok, which receive the website events described above and may use them under their own policies to measure and target ads.
  • GitHub Pages: standard web request logs, to host this website and the app.

Apple Health

Apple Health is off until you connect it in Settings, and Apple asks you which data Polaris may read and write. We use what you allow only to log your workouts and weight in Polaris and to save your lifting sessions back to Apple Health. Data from Apple Health:

  • is never used for advertising, marketing or ad measurement, and is never sent to any advertising or analytics partner (Meta, Google's ad and analytics tools, TikTok or Mixpanel);
  • is never sold, and never shared with data brokers;
  • is stored with the rest of your log (Supabase), and the parts a Coach, Physio, food or recap request needs go to our AI provider to answer you, as described above. Nobody else gets it, except as required by law;
  • is never stored in iCloud by us.

You can stop it any time in the iPhone's Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Polaris, or in Polaris Settings → Apple Health. Deleting your account deletes what Polaris saved; it doesn't delete anything in Apple Health.

Friends: depending on your settings, friends see your workouts (muscles worked, time, sets, total moved, personal records and exercise names), your weekly points and level, and your lift details (the weights on your workouts and your best set for each lift each month). All of this is on by default, and each part has its own switch. They don't see your food, body weight, pain, rehab, pregnancy details or Coach chats. You can turn sharing off, or set any workout to Only me. Comments you write are seen by everyone who can see that post. When you finish a week on top (for example #1 among your friends or in your crew), your friends and crew see your badge next to your name that week, and anything you choose to post about it.

Crews: people in a crew with you see how many sessions you did this week and, if you share lift details, your week's total for the crew's lift challenge.

Finding you by name: anyone signed in to Polaris can find you in Add friends by typing at least 3 letters of your display name or @handle, and sees your display name, @handle and profile photo in the results, plus whether you're already friends or have a request between you. This is on by default. Your friends profile is made when you sign up, from the first word of the name your sign-in gives us (Google or Apple) and its photo, with an @handle made from it; change either on You. Add friends also suggests people you may know before anyone types: people with friends in common first, then other people who can be found. Turn off "Let people find me by name" on You and only someone who types your exact @handle can find you, and you're never suggested. People you've blocked, or who blocked you, never see you there.

Public pages and share links: your page at polarisbody.com/@yourhandle shows your display name and photo to anyone who opens it. When you share a card (a finished workout, a personal record, a food day or a monthly recap), anyone with that link can see the card with your name and photo, and we count how many times it's opened. A card shows only its numbers, such as sets, time, total moved, a lift's weight and reps, or a day's protein and calories. It never includes text you typed, your body weight, pain, rehab or pregnancy details. A share link also works as your friend invite: someone with a Polaris account who opens it can add you as a friend without asking you first (up to 10 people per invite), and then sees what your sharing settings show friends. Share links stop working after 30 days.

Legal requests: we disclose data only when the law requires it and only in response to valid legal process. Where we're allowed to, we tell you first. We will not provide information about reproductive health care, including pregnancy and its outcome, to help investigate care that was lawful where it happened.

Business transfer: if RISE LLC or Polaris is sold or merged, your data may transfer to the new owner under this policy.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is open. Deleting your account removes your data from our database right away. Feature-use events are kept for 13 months, session replays for 30 days, Coach mistake samples for up to 30 days, and error reports for up to 90 days. Email data is kept until you unsubscribe or delete your account. Notification records are kept for 90 days. Reports, and the content they're about, are kept for up to a year after we resolve them, to handle repeat abuse, even if the content is deleted. Purchase records are kept as long as tax and accounting law requires, even after you delete your account. Backups are overwritten on a rolling schedule.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete everything: in the app, open You and tap Delete my account and data. Everything stored with us is removed right away, including usage analytics. The copy at Mixpanel carries only a random ID. Ask us and we'll delete it there within 30 days. Your Loops contact is deleted automatically. Deleting your account doesn't cancel a Pro subscription: cancel it in your iPhone's Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or at apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions.
  • Get a copy, or correct your data: edit it in the app, or email us.
  • Turn things off: usage analytics and session replays (Settings → Data and privacy), each kind of notification by push and by email (Settings, or your phone's notification settings), who can comment on your posts, sharing with friends and being found by name (You), any single workout (Only me), and Apple Health (Settings → Apple Health, or the iPhone's Health settings).
  • California residents: you have the right to know what we collect, to delete it, to correct it, to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information (Your privacy choices, or a Global Privacy Control signal), to limit the use of sensitive personal information, and not to be treated differently for using these rights. We don't sell or share sensitive personal information, including health data.
  • Residents of other states with privacy laws (for example Washington, Nevada, Connecticut, Colorado, Virginia, Oregon and Texas) have similar rights.

To make a request, email support@polarisbody.com with the subject "Privacy request". We answer within 45 days and may need to confirm it's you. If we deny your request, you can appeal by replying "Appeal".

Children

Polaris is for adults 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from children under 13. If we learn that we have, we delete it.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Database rules let only your own signed-in account read your data. The Coach's instructions and our keys stay on our server. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data, we'll notify you and regulators as the law requires.

Changes

We'll tell you in the app or by email before material changes to this policy take effect.
