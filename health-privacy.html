Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 November 2026 · Version 7. This policy covers consumer health data under Washington's My Health My Data Act, Nevada law and similar laws. It adds to our Privacy Policy. Polaris is made by RISE LLC, 13157 Mindanao Way, PMB 127, Marina Del Rey, CA 90292 · support@polarisbody.com.

Consumer health data we collect

Body measurements (weight, waist, body fat %); aches, pain reports and rehab progress; pregnancy, due date, birth date, breastfeeding and pregnancy-ended status; food and nutrition logs and meal photos; exercise and activity data, including workouts and body weight you let Polaris read from Apple Health and training history you import from another app; Coach chats about any of these, and the notes the Coach keeps between chats; and what we work out from them, for example your food targets or a likely training-related pattern behind an ache.

Where it comes from

From you, from Apple Health if you connect it in the iPhone app, and from calculations we make from what you enter.

How we use it

  • To provide the features you ask for: plans, targets, the Coach, Physio and your progress.
  • To keep targets within safety limits.
  • To fix errors.
  • To improve the Coach, by reviewing chats with names and contact details removed, as described in our Privacy Policy.

Who we share it with

Only our service providers, which process it for us: Supabase (hosting), our AI provider, Google (Gemini API), Sentry (only feedback notes you choose to send), and Loops (our email service: only which steps you've reached in the app, such as finished setup, first workout or not logged in a while, to send you getting-started emails, and for notification emails the kind of update, a friend's display name or that you were #1 that week; never what you logged). Google uses it only to answer your Coach, Physio, food and monthly recap requests and to match an exercise name you type, and not to train its models. It gets nothing until you allow AI in the app, and you can turn it off any time in Settings → Privacy → AI coach. Friends see workouts only as your sharing settings allow, and never your food, body weight, pain, rehab, pregnancy details or Coach chats. If you choose to share a card, anyone with its link sees only that card's numbers (for example a food day's protein and calories), never your body weight, pain, rehab or pregnancy details. A share link also works as your friend invite: someone with a Polaris account who opens it can add you as a friend without asking you first (up to 10 people per invite), and then sees what your sharing settings show friends. Share links stop working after 30 days. We do not sell consumer health data. Data from Apple Health is never shared with advertisers or used for advertising, even if you opt in to anything else.

Advertising

Our marketing website uses advertising and analytics tags (Meta, Google and TikTok), on by default for US visitors unless you opt out (Your privacy choices, or a Global Privacy Control signal), and only after you accept on our recovery page and for visitors in Europe. They only see website activity such as pages visited and whether you joined the waitlist. Consumer health data from the app is never sent to those tags or to any advertising partner, and never used to target ads, unless you give separate, explicit opt-in consent for that specific purpose. We would only ever sell consumer health data with your signed, valid authorization as the law requires, which you can revoke at any time. You can withdraw any consent in the app or by emailing us.

Your rights

  • Confirm whether we collect, share or sell your consumer health data, and get a list of every third party and affiliate we've shared it with, with their contact details.
  • Get a copy of your consumer health data.
  • Delete it, including copies held by our service providers. You can do this in the app: open You and tap Delete my account and data.
  • Withdraw your consent to our collecting or sharing it. Some features may stop working.

Email support@polarisbody.com with the subject "Health data request". We respond within 45 days. If we deny your request, you can appeal by replying "Appeal". If your appeal is denied, you can contact the Washington State Attorney General.

Geofencing

We don't set up virtual boundaries around places where people get health care.
