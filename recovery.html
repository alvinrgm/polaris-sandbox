Questions

Before you start.

Does it replace my PT or doctor?

No. Polaris works alongside your PT or doctor and keeps your rehab going every day between visits.

Where do the coach's suggestions come from?

Your logged sets, meals, weigh-ins and tapped aches, plus your goal. It also follows set rules, like adding reps before weight, never going below a safe calorie floor, and moving through rehab in phases.

What if my pain gets worse?

Get it checked by your doctor or PT, especially if pain turns sharp, stays constant at rest or doesn't improve.
